Orbura

Daily check-in

A 45-second camera check-in that listens to your breathing and pulse — measured entirely on this device.

  • On-device only. Frames never leave this Mac. The SDK processes video locally; the only network call is key validation.
  • Nothing is stored. No images, no video, no landmark data is saved or uploaded.
  • You control it. Stop anytime. The camera light is your indicator it's live.
  • Wellness, not medicine. These are informational signals, not a diagnosis.

Powered by the Presage SmartSpectra SDK.
